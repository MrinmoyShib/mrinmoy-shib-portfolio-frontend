# Authentic screenshots and marking demonstration

The assignment requires **real screenshots of your working website**, not placeholder renders. This code delivery does not include screenshots fabricated from sample data; capture them after you enter your own work.

Put at least four screenshots into `screenshots/` and link them in `README.md`:

1. `home-light.png`: entire home page with real profile avatar, skills, three real featured projects and three published articles.
2. `home-dark.png`: same homepage with the header's theme switch set to dark.
3. `blog-list.png`: published articles and working filters.
4. `blog-detail.png`: article, tags, likes and approved comments.
5. `owner-dashboard.png`: dashboard charts/cards with real or clearly marked coursework-test interactions.

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |

Record the real UI in a browser with address bar visible where relevant. The recording link must be accessible to your grader. Avoid including personal secret passwords or tokens in the capture.
